Delete your Prep4CNA account

This page is for the Prep4CNA app. It explains how to delete your account and exactly what is deleted and what is kept.

Contact
info@ebold.tech

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Prep4CNA and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Find the Account section and tap Delete account.
  3. Read the message and tap Delete account again to confirm.
  4. Sign in once more with the same Google or Apple account when asked. This proves the account is yours.
  5. The account is deleted and you are signed out.

Can't open the app or can't sign in?

Email info@ebold.tech from the email address of the account you want deleted, with the subject Delete my Prep4CNA account. We will delete it and reply to confirm, within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account record in our sign-in system (Firebase Authentication): your account ID, email address, name and profile photo (if Google provided them), and your sign-in method and dates. This is deleted immediately and permanently.
  • For accounts created with Apple, the Sign in with Apple connection to Prep4CNA is revoked, so it also disappears from your Apple ID settings.

What is not deleted, and for how long

  • Quiz progress on your phone (answers, streak, exam results, saved jobs). It was never uploaded and is not linked to your account. It stays on the phone until you tap Profile → Reset Progress or uninstall the app.
  • Old leaderboard entries (a nickname and three score numbers) made in app versions before 1.3.0. They are not tied to your sign-in account. To have yours removed, email us your nickname and we will delete it.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase). They are tied to an anonymous per-install identifier, not to your name, email or account, and are kept by Google under its standard analytics retention settings. Uninstalling the app stops new data from being collected.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
