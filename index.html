Prep4CNA — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026 App version: 1.3.0

Prep4CNA is a multilingual (currently English + Haitian Creole) study app for Nursing Assistant exam prep. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have — for every person who installs it, whether or not you sign in. Where something changed in version 1.3.0, the text says which versions it applies to.

Independent, unofficial app

Prep4CNA is an independent study and practice tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or a representative of any government agency, state Board of Nursing, state Department of Health, or official CNA certification body. CNA certification exams are administered under the federally mandated Nurse Aide Training and Competency Evaluation Program (NATCEP); official program information is available from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). For official exam registration and your state's specific requirements, contact your state's Board of Nursing or Department of Health directly.

Scope of this policy

This policy covers the Prep4CNA mobile app, available on iOS and Android, and applies to everyone who installs it. It does not cover any other app, website, or service.

2

Information we collect

2.1 — Stored only on your device

As you use the app, your answers to quiz questions, your accuracy, your daily streak, and your Exam Simulator results are saved in local storage on your phone. This information stays on your device. It is not linked to your account (Section 2.2) and is not uploaded.

2.2 — Your account (version 1.3.0 and later)

Home and the Daily Quiz work without an account. To use the rest of the app (topic quizzes, the Exam Simulator, Vocabulary, Jobs, Clinical Skills videos and Profile) you sign in with Google or, on iPhone, Apple. Google or Apple checks who you are and tells Firebase Authentication (the Google service we use to run sign-in) — we never see, ask for or store your password. Firebase keeps this record for your account:

We do not use this information for advertising or marketing, and we do not sell it (Section 5). Your quiz answers, streak and exam results are not linked to your account — they stay on your phone (Section 2.1). You can sign out, or delete the account completely, from Profile → Account (Section 6). Versions before 1.3.0 have no sign-in screen; they create the anonymous identifier described in Section 2.3.

Versions before 1.3.0 had an optional leaderboard. Those versions gave each device a random, anonymous identifier (through Firebase Anonymous Authentication) so the device could update its own score, and people who picked a nickname shared the following with others using those versions:

Your individual quiz answers and your device identifier were never part of them. To have your entry removed, write to us with your nickname (Section 11).

2.4 — Basic usage analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, in aggregate, how the app is actually used — which screens people visit, how long a typical session lasts, and which features get used most. This happens automatically for every install; there's currently no in-app toggle to turn it off. It's tied to an anonymous per-install identifier, separate from your account (Section 2.2), and is not linked to your name or email.

This analytics collection does not include an advertising identifier (Apple's IDFA or Google's Advertising ID) — we've specifically configured it not to. No ads are shown in the app, and this data is not used for advertising, ad personalization, or shared onward with anyone for their own purposes.

2.5 — Push notifications (optional)

Notifications are off until you turn them on from Profile → Notifications, and your phone's own permission prompt appears first. If you do turn them on, Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your phone a push registration token so a message can reach it, and your phone is subscribed to a single shared "announcements" topic — so everyone who opts in receives the same messages (for example, a new app version or important CNA exam news). We don't store this token in our own database, we don't keep a list of who has notifications on, and we can't send a message to one particular person. Notifications are delivered through Apple's and Google's own push services. You can turn them off any time from Profile → Notifications or in your phone's settings.

2.6 — Job listings

The Jobs tab shows recent CNA job listings supplied by Adzuna (adzuna.com). Our own systems collect the listings and store them in our database; your phone only reads them, so nothing about you is sent to Adzuna to get them. The state you pick, the jobs you save, and the lists you've recently viewed are kept on your device. Picking a state is just a choice from a list — the app never reads your location.

Two things do involve other companies directly:

  • The Adzuna logo shown on each listing is loaded from Adzuna's content delivery servers, so those servers receive your device's IP address and the usual technical details of any web request.
  • Tapping a listing opens Adzuna's page — and then usually the employer's own site — in a browser window inside the app. Those sites are run by other companies under their own privacy policies, and we don't receive or see what you do on them.
Stay safe

Prep4CNA does not employ, and cannot vouch for, the employers in these listings. Never pay anyone to apply for a job, to be hired, or for training or equipment — a real employer will not ask you to.

2.7 — Reporting a job listing

Each listing has a Report link. It opens your own email app with a message to us, pre-filled with the listing's ID, title, employer, place and link. The message is sent from your email account, so we receive your email address and anything you choose to add. We use it only to review the listing and, if needed, reply to you.

2.8 — Clinical Skills videos (YouTube)

The Clinical Skills videos are hosted on YouTube. When you open one, the app loads YouTube's player inside the app, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced address (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube and Google therefore receive your device's IP address and the usual technical details of any web request, and once you press play they may store or read cookies or similar identifiers under Google's privacy policy. We don't receive what you watch there. Each video credits the person who made it.

3

Information we do not collect

The app does not collect, request, or have access to:

  • Your phone number or home address
  • Your password — Google or Apple handles the sign-in and we never see it
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Advertising identifiers (Apple's IDFA or Google's Advertising ID) — we've specifically turned this off, and the app shows no advertising
  • Crash logs or diagnostic data

We do collect basic, anonymous usage analytics — see Section 2.4 for exactly what that includes and why it's separate from everything else on this list. The account details we do receive when you sign in are listed in Section 2.2. If you send us an email to report a job listing (Section 2.7), we receive the email address you send it from.

4

How we use information

The limited information described in Section 2 is used only to:

  • Save your quiz progress and streak between sessions, on your own device
  • Show quiz content (questions, categories, app text) that stays current without needing an app update
  • Let you sign in and keep the parts of the app that need an account behind it (Section 2.2)
  • Show a required-update notice if a critical fix is ever released
  • Send the push notifications you've chosen to turn on (Section 2.5)
  • Show job listings for the state you pick, and remember your saved jobs on your device (Section 2.6)
  • Review a job listing you've reported to us (Section 2.7)
  • Play the Clinical Skills videos you choose to open (Section 2.8)
  • Understand overall app usage (Section 2.4), so we know what's working and what to fix or improve next
5

Who we share information with

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The app's content, sign-in accounts, saved leaderboard entries, and usage analytics (Section 2.4) all run on Google Firebase, which acts as our infrastructure provider — the same way a filing cabinet stores paper without reading it. Firebase's own privacy terms are available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Job listings come from Adzuna, and the pages you open from them belong to Adzuna and to employers (Section 2.6). Sign-in goes through Google or Apple, who learn that you signed in to Prep4CNA, as with any app that offers their sign-in (Section 2.2). Skills videos are played by YouTube (Section 2.8). Push notifications travel through Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple's and Google's push services (Section 2.5). We do not give any of these companies your quiz progress, exam results, or anything else described above as staying on your device.

6

Your choices & controls

  • Clear your local progress any time from Profile → Reset Progress. This immediately and permanently erases your saved answers, streak and exam results from your device.
  • Turn notifications off any time from Profile → Notifications, or in your phone's settings.
  • Remove a saved job by tapping its bookmark again. Saved jobs and your chosen state are also erased when you uninstall the app.
  • Sign out any time from Profile → Account.
  • Delete your account any time from Profile → Account → Delete account. You confirm with Google or Apple once more, and the account record in Firebase is permanently deleted (for Apple accounts, the Sign in with Apple link is revoked too). Your answers on the phone are not touched; use Reset Progress for those.
  • Request removal of an old leaderboard entry by contacting us (Section 11) with your nickname. We'll remove it.
Being upfront

Deleting your account is self-service, inside the app. Old leaderboard entries from versions before 1.3.0 are different: they can only be removed by contacting us directly. We process removal requests promptly.

7

Data retention

Local progress is retained on your device until you reset it or uninstall the app. Saved jobs, your chosen state and recently viewed job lists stay on your device until you remove them or uninstall the app. Your account record is kept until you delete your account (Section 6). Old leaderboard entries are kept until you ask us to remove yours (Section 6) or we decide to delete them all. Emails you send to report a job listing are kept only as long as needed to review the listing.

8

Children's privacy

Prep4CNA is designed for adults preparing for professional exam prep and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Signing in requires a Google or Apple account, which have their own age requirements; if we learn that a child under 13 has an account, we'll delete it.

9

Security

Sign-in is handled by Google and Apple through Firebase Authentication; the app never sees your password. Quiz content and job listings are readable by the app but not editable by it, and access rules limit what the app can write to our database. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but the account details we hold are limited to the items in Section 2.2.
