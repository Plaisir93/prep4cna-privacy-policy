Prep4CNA — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026 App version: 1.2.0

Prep4CNA is a multilingual (currently English + Haitian Creole) study app for Nursing Assistant exam prep. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have — for every person who installs it, whether or not you ever create a leaderboard nickname.

Independent, unofficial app

Prep4CNA is an independent study and practice tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or a representative of any government agency, state Board of Nursing, state Department of Health, or official CNA certification body. CNA certification exams are administered under the federally mandated Nurse Aide Training and Competency Evaluation Program (NATCEP); official program information is available from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). For official exam registration and your state's specific requirements, contact your state's Board of Nursing or Department of Health directly.

No account, ever
No email, password, or real name is asked for or required to use the app.
No ads, no ad tracking
We use basic, anonymous usage analytics to improve the app — never ads or ad-tracking identifiers.
Answers stay on your phone
Your quiz history, streak, and progress are stored only on your device.
Leaderboard is opt-in
A public nickname and score are only shared if you choose to join the leaderboard.
Notifications are opt-in
Nothing is sent to your phone unless you turn notifications on, and you can turn them off any time.
Job links open other websites
Listings come from Adzuna. Tapping one opens a site we don't control, so we say so plainly (Section 2.6).

Scope of this policy

This policy covers the Prep4CNA mobile app, available on iOS and Android, and applies to everyone who installs it. It does not cover any other app, website, or service.

2

Information we collect

2.1 — Stored only on your device

As you use the app, your answers to quiz questions, your accuracy, your daily streak, and your Exam Simulator results are saved in local storage on your phone. This information never leaves your device unless you choose to join the leaderboard (see 2.3).

2.2 — An anonymous device identifier

The app assigns your device a random, anonymous identifier (via Firebase Anonymous Authentication) the first time it needs one. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal information, and there is no sign-in screen — it happens automatically in the background. It exists so that, if you join the leaderboard, your device can update its own score without anyone else being able to update it for you.

2.3 — Information you choose to share: the leaderboard

Joining the leaderboard is optional and requires one deliberate action: picking a nickname. If you do, the following becomes visible to anyone using the app:

2.4 — Basic usage analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand, in aggregate, how the app is actually used — which screens people visit, how long a typical session lasts, and which features get used most. This happens automatically for every install; there's currently no in-app toggle to turn it off. It's tied to an anonymous per-install identifier, separate from the one described in Section 2.2, and is never linked to a name, email, or any other information that could identify you.

This analytics collection does not include an advertising identifier (Apple's IDFA or Google's Advertising ID) — we've specifically configured it not to. No ads are shown in the app, and this data is not used for advertising, ad personalization, or shared onward with anyone for their own purposes.

2.5 — Push notifications (optional)

Notifications are off until you turn them on from Profile → Notifications, and your phone's own permission prompt appears first. If you do turn them on, Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your phone a push registration token so a message can reach it, and your phone is subscribed to a single shared "announcements" topic — so everyone who opts in receives the same messages (for example, a new app version or important CNA exam news). We don't store this token in our own database, we don't keep a list of who has notifications on, and we can't send a message to one particular person. Notifications are delivered through Apple's and Google's own push services. You can turn them off any time from Profile → Notifications or in your phone's settings.

2.6 — Job listings

The Jobs tab shows recent CNA job listings supplied by Adzuna (adzuna.com). Our own systems collect the listings and store them in our database; your phone only reads them, so nothing about you is sent to Adzuna to get them. The state you pick, the jobs you save, and the lists you've recently viewed are kept on your device. Picking a state is just a choice from a list — the app never reads your location.

Two things do involve other companies directly:

  • The Adzuna logo shown on each listing is loaded from Adzuna's content delivery servers, so those servers receive your device's IP address and the usual technical details of any web request.
  • Tapping a listing opens Adzuna's page — and then usually the employer's own site — in a browser window inside the app. Those sites are run by other companies under their own privacy policies, and we don't receive or see what you do on them.
Stay safe

Prep4CNA does not employ, and cannot vouch for, the employers in these listings. Never pay anyone to apply for a job, to be hired, or for training or equipment — a real employer will not ask you to.

2.7 — Reporting a job listing

Each listing has a Report link. It opens your own email app with a message to us, pre-filled with the listing's ID, title, employer, place and link. The message is sent from your email account, so we receive your email address and anything you choose to add. We use it only to review the listing and, if needed, reply to you.

3

Information we do not collect

The app does not collect, request, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Advertising identifiers (Apple's IDFA or Google's Advertising ID) — we've specifically turned this off, and the app shows no advertising
  • Crash logs or diagnostic data

We do collect basic, anonymous usage analytics — see Section 2.4 for exactly what that includes and why it's separate from everything else on this list. If you send us an email to report a job listing (Section 2.7), we receive the email address you send it from.

4

How we use information

The limited information described in Section 2 is used only to:

  • Save your quiz progress and streak between sessions, on your own device
  • Show quiz content (questions, categories, app text) that stays current without needing an app update
  • Display leaderboard rankings, only for people who've opted in
  • Show a required-update notice if a critical fix is ever released
  • Send the push notifications you've chosen to turn on (Section 2.5)
  • Show job listings for the state you pick, and remember your saved jobs on your device (Section 2.6)
  • Review a job listing you've reported to us (Section 2.7)
  • Understand overall app usage (Section 2.4), so we know what's working and what to fix or improve next
5

Who we share information with

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The app's content, optional leaderboard, and usage analytics (Section 2.4) all run on Google Firebase, which acts as our infrastructure provider — the same way a filing cabinet stores paper without reading it. Firebase's own privacy terms are available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Job listings come from Adzuna, and the pages you open from them belong to Adzuna and to employers (Section 2.6). Push notifications travel through Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple's and Google's push services (Section 2.5). We do not give any of these companies your quiz progress, exam results, leaderboard data, or anything else described above as staying on your device.

6

Your choices & controls

  • Clear your local progress any time from Profile → Reset Progress. This immediately and permanently erases your saved answers, streak and exam results from your device.
  • Turn notifications off any time from Profile → Notifications, or in your phone's settings.
  • Remove a saved job by tapping its bookmark again. Saved jobs and your chosen state are also erased when you uninstall the app.
  • Leave the leaderboard off entirely by simply never setting a nickname — nothing about your device is ever shared publicly.
  • Request removal of your leaderboard entry by contacting us (Section 11) with your nickname. We'll remove it.
Being upfront

Leaderboard entries can currently only be removed by contacting us directly — there isn't yet a self-service delete button inside the app for a leaderboard entry specifically (local progress reset is instant and self-service, per above). We process removal requests promptly.

7

Data retention

Local progress is retained on your device until you reset it or uninstall the app. Saved jobs, your chosen state and recently viewed job lists stay on your device until you remove them or uninstall the app. Leaderboard entries are retained until you request removal (Section 6) or the leaderboard feature is discontinued. Emails you send to report a job listing are kept only as long as needed to review the listing.

8

Children's privacy

Prep4CNA is designed for adults preparing for professional exam prep and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9

Security

Leaderboard data is protected by access rules that only allow a device to write its own entry, verified through the anonymous identifier described in Section 2.2 — no one can edit or impersonate another person's score. Quiz content and job listings are readable by the app but not editable by it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we don't collect anything more sensitive than a self-chosen nickname and a score.

10

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. If a future change meaningfully expands what we collect, we'll also note it in the app's release notes.
