Prep4CNA — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026 App version: 1.0.0

Prep4CNA is a multilingual (currently English + Haitian Creole) study app for Nursing Assistant exam prep. This page explains, in plain language, what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have — for every person who installs it, whether or not you ever create a leaderboard nickname.

Independent, unofficial app

Prep4CNA is an independent study and practice tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or a representative of any government agency, state Board of Nursing, state Department of Health, or official CNA certification body. CNA certification exams are administered under the federally mandated Nurse Aide Training and Competency Evaluation Program (NATCEP); official program information is available from the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). For official exam registration and your state's specific requirements, contact your state's Board of Nursing or Department of Health directly.

No account, ever
No email, password, or real name is asked for or required to use the app.
No ads, no trackers
No advertising network, no analytics SDK, no third-party tracking of any kind.
Answers stay on your phone
Your quiz history, streak, and progress are stored only on your device.
Leaderboard is opt-in
A public nickname and score are only shared if you choose to join the leaderboard.

1

Scope of this policy

This policy covers the Prep4CNA mobile app, available on iOS and Android, and applies to everyone who installs it. It does not cover any other app, website, or service.

2

Information we collect

2.1 — Stored only on your device

As you use the app, your answers to quiz questions, your accuracy, and your daily streak are saved in local storage on your phone. This information never leaves your device unless you choose to join the leaderboard (see 2.3).

2.2 — An anonymous device identifier

The app assigns your device a random, anonymous identifier (via Firebase Anonymous Authentication) the first time it needs one. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal information, and there is no sign-in screen — it happens automatically in the background. It exists so that, if you join the leaderboard, your device can update its own score without anyone else being able to update it for you.

2.3 — Information you choose to share: the leaderboard

Joining the leaderboard is optional and requires one deliberate action: picking a nickname. If you do, the following becomes visible to anyone using the app:

3

Information we do not collect

The app does not collect, request, or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Advertising identifiers, and it shows no advertising
  • Analytics or crash-tracking data of any kind — no third-party analytics SDK is included in the app
4

How we use information

The limited information described in Section 2 is used only to:

  • Save your quiz progress and streak between sessions, on your own device
  • Show quiz content (questions, categories, app text) that stays current without needing an app update
  • Display leaderboard rankings, only for people who've opted in
  • Show a required-update notice if a critical fix is ever released
5

Who we share information with

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The app's content and optional leaderboard are hosted on Google Firebase, which acts as our infrastructure provider — the same way a filing cabinet stores paper without reading it. Firebase's own privacy terms are available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

6

Your choices & controls

  • Clear your local progress any time from Profile → Reset Progress. This immediately and permanently erases your saved answers and streak from your device.
  • Leave the leaderboard off entirely by simply never setting a nickname — nothing about your device is ever shared publicly.
  • Request removal of your leaderboard entry by contacting us (Section 11) with your nickname. We'll remove it.
Being upfront

Leaderboard entries can currently only be removed by contacting us directly — there isn't yet a self-service delete button inside the app for a leaderboard entry specifically (local progress reset is instant and self-service, per above). We process removal requests promptly.

7

Data retention

Local progress is retained on your device until you reset it or uninstall the app. Leaderboard entries are retained until you request removal (Section 6) or the leaderboard feature is discontinued.

8

Children's privacy

Prep4CNA is designed for adults preparing for professional exam prep and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9

Security

Leaderboard data is protected by access rules that only allow a device to write its own entry, verified through the anonymous identifier described in Section 2.2 — no one can edit or impersonate another person's score. Quiz content is readable by the app but not editable by it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we don't collect anything more sensitive than a self-chosen nickname and a score.

10

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. If a future change meaningfully expands what we collect, we'll also note it in the app's release notes.

11

Contact us
