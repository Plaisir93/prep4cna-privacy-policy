Common questions

How do I reset my progress?
Go to the Profile tab → Reset Progress. This clears your saved answers and streak from your device immediately.

I think a question or answer is wrong — what do I do?
Email us at the address above with the question text (or a screenshot) and we'll review it. Quiz content updates automatically once corrected, no app update needed.

How do I remove my leaderboard entry?
Email us with your leaderboard nickname and we'll remove it.

Does the app work offline?
You'll need an internet connection to load quiz content, but your progress is saved locally on your device.
